# CraveGo – UI/UX Design Internship

A 14-day UI/UX design project developed as part of my internship.

The project follows the complete UI/UX design process, starting from research and user flows and progressing through wireframing, design systems, high-fidelity UI, prototyping, and final presentation.

## Project

CraveGo is a food delivery mobile application concept designed with a focus on simple navigation, clear visual hierarchy, and a user-friendly ordering experience.

## Internship Progress

- Day 1 – UI/UX Introduction
- Day 2 – User Research
- Day 3 – User Journey & User Flow
- Day 4 – Low-Fidelity Wireframes
- Day 5 – Design System
- Day 6 – High-Fidelity UI
- Day 7 – Home & Restaurant Screens
- Day 8 – Product, Cart & Checkout
- Day 9 – Profile, Orders & Settings
- Day 10 – Interactive Prototype
- Day 11 – Usability Testing
- Day 12 – Design Improvements
- Day 13 – Final UI Refinement
- Day 14 – Final Presentation
