## 📅 Internship

**Organization:** Codomax Digital Solutions

**Project:** CraveGo – Food Delivery Mobile Application

**Task:** Day 2 – User Research

---

# 📖 Introduction

The second day of the internship focused on **User Research**, which forms the foundation of every successful UI/UX design process. Before creating wireframes or user interfaces, it is essential to understand who the users are, what they expect from the application, and the challenges they face while completing their tasks.

The objective of this activity was to gather insights about potential users of the CraveGo Food Delivery Mobile Application and transform those insights into meaningful design decisions.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Identify the primary and secondary users of the application.
- Understand user goals and expectations.
- Analyze common problems experienced while ordering food online.
- Create user personas representing different user groups.
- Map the user journey from opening the application to tracking an order.

---

# 👥 Target Users

### Primary Users

- College Students
- Working Professionals
- Families
- Individuals living alone

### Secondary Users

- Restaurants
- Delivery Partners

---

# 💡 User Needs

The research identified several important user requirements.

- Easy account registration
- Quick restaurant search
- Clear food images
- Secure online payment
- Fast checkout
- Live order tracking
- Order history
- Favourite restaurants
- Discounts and offers
- Simple navigation

---

# ⚠️ Pain Points

The following challenges are commonly experienced by users while using food delivery applications.

- Lengthy checkout process
- Difficult navigation
- Slow application performance
- Hidden delivery charges
- Poor search functionality
- Lack of order tracking
- Confusing interface
- No personalized recommendations

---
