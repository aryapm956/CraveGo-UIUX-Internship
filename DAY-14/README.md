🍔 CraveGo – Food Delivery Mobile Application UI/UX

CraveGo is a modern and user-friendly food delivery mobile application designed in Figma as part of my UI/UX Design Internship at Codomax Digital Solutions.

The project follows the complete UI/UX workflow, from user research and wireframing to a fully interactive high-fidelity prototype. It focuses on delivering a seamless food ordering experience through clean layouts, intuitive navigation, reusable components, and a consistent design system.
