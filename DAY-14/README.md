🍔 CraveGo – Food Delivery Mobile Application UI/UX

CraveGo is a modern, user-centered Food Delivery Mobile Application designed in Figma as part of my UI/UX Design Internship at Codomax Digital Solutions.

This project follows the complete UI/UX Design Process, beginning with user research and ending with a fully interactive prototype. The goal was to create an intuitive, visually appealing, and seamless food ordering experience through thoughtful user-centered design.
